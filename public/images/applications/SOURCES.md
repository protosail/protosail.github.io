# Application imagery

These illustrations are artistic depth-map interpretations of online photographs, edited with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. They are not measured depth data or photographs of Protosail deployments. JPEGs are 1536 × 1024, exported at quality 88. Source photos, full-resolution generated PNGs and exact prompts are retained in `output/application-sources/`.

Treatment: use charcoal-to-silver depth shading and highlight selected cranes, the environmental survey boat, the nearest wind turbine and the observed cargo vessel in the website's orange accent (#ff6a2b). The environmental image is a generated composition; the other three preserve their source photograph's scene composition.

Copy presents potential future applications with task-specific payloads; it does not assert that the current student prototype has these capabilities. Context checked against [NOAA's overview of uncrewed surface vessels](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/technology/usv/) and [Saildrone's offshore wind applications](https://www.saildrone.com/news/ocean-drones-enable-offshore-wind-farms). Close-contact underwater inspection, NDT, millimetric surveys and active mine/anti-submarine capabilities are not claimed.
