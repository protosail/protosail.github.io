    Ocean autonomy, applied.

    Beyond the Atlantic crossing, the technology we develop could support a wide range of maritime missions, with sensors and payloads tailored to each task.

    Ports & coastal infrastructure

    Repeat coastal surveys, water-quality measurements and vessel tracking along open approaches to ports.

    Environmental monitoring & restoration

    Track water quality and marine life over time, helping researchers understand ecosystem health and assess restoration efforts.

    Offshore energy

    Collect wind, wave and current data to support offshore site planning, environmental assessments and vessel monitoring.

    Maritime security

    Extend awareness across remote waters through vessel tracking, passive acoustic sensing and observations relayed to shore.
